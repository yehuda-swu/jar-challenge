THE BIG IDEA

What matters most?

Put the big rocks first.

Our time and energy are limited, just like the space in the jar. When we fill our time with small things first, the important things may no longer fit.

Rocks · Your biggest priorities

Health, family, values and meaningful goals.

Stones · Other important things

Schoolwork, commitments and responsibilities.

Pebbles · Smaller things

Scrolling, distractions and everyday tasks. These can still matter, but should not crowd out your priorities.

Think about your own jar

What are your three big rocks? What could you give less time to this week?

The categories are personal. What matters most to one person may be different for another.
